# About 

## Introduction
Kevin is a PhD student at the University of Technology Sydney’s Behavioural Data Science Lab. His research focuses on how information spreads online and how to make machine learning models more robust in messy real-world settings. Before the PhD, he studied at the University of Sydney—earning an Honours degree, a University Medal, and deepening his understanding by teaching data science and machine learning. Outside academia he carries a film camera, hunts for new coffee shops, trains, rotates hobbies like archery, and enjoys sharing travel tips or the best flat white in Sydney.

## Research Focus
- Information diffusion & online narrative dynamics
- Dynamic & temporal graph learning on social platforms
- Multimodal representation learning for text, images & video
- Misinformation & disinformation modelling
