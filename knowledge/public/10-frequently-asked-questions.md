# Frequently asked questions

## Who is Kevin Zheng?

Kevin is a Sydney-based PhD candidate at UTS in the Behavioural Data Science Lab. He researches information diffusion, misinformation, social data science, and robust machine learning.

## What is Kevin working on now?

His current PhD direction studies how information and narratives spread online and how machine-learning methods can model misinformation and disinformation under real-world data complexity.

## Who supervises Kevin's PhD?

His primary supervisor is Dr Marian-Andrei Rizoiu, and Dr Lin Tian is his co-supervisor.

## What did Kevin study?

He completed a Bachelor of Advanced Computing (Honours) in Computational Data Science and a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics at the University of Sydney. He also studied Mathematics and Machine Learning Theory on exchange at ETH Zurich.

## What is EchoAlign?

EchoAlign is Kevin's noisy-label learning research. Instead of trying only to correct a questionable label, it uses controlled generative models to modify an ambiguous input so that it better aligns with the observed label, then retains reliable original examples to reduce distribution shift.

## Does Kevin teach?

Yes. He remains a Casual Academic at the University of Sydney and has taught Advanced Machine Learning and Informatics: Data and Computation. He has also delivered a guest lecture about LLMs and GPT architecture.

## Is Kevin open to collaboration?

Yes. His public website states that he is open to research collaborations, talks, and teaching ideas, especially around information diffusion and robust machine learning.

## What does Kevin do outside academia?

His public profile mentions film photography, coffee, the gym, archery, travel, and interest in activities such as diving and skydiving.
