# Reading Too Much into Context: Passive Exposure Can Steer LLM Decisions

## Publication record

- Authors: Yuxiang Zheng, Lin Tian, and Marian-Andrei Rizoiu.
- Status: arXiv preprint; first submitted on 27 September 2026.
- Paper and source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33065

## Research summary

The study compares LLM decisions with and without external content that offers no reason to change the decision. It finds shifts across the tested open-weight and closed-weight models, including with real-world online opinions. Effects extend to violations of explicit user requirements and greater acceptance of false claims.

These findings describe the models and tasks tested. The arXiv record does not establish conference acceptance or journal publication; do not present the preprint as peer-reviewed or invent a code or dataset release.
